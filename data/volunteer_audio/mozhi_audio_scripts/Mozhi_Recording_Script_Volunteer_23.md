# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 23

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- Someone should check if the replication lag is affecting reporting.
- The reporting server keeps running out of temp space during the export.
- The scheduled job overlapped with the backup window and locked the table.
- The build broke because a dependency wasn't resolved correctly.
- We think the outage was regional, not just our data center.
- Can you confirm the new hire's badge access was set up correctly?
- Our uptime dropped below the promised threshold this quarter.
- That's actually the third time this exact ticket has been reopened.
- Someone needs to close out that incident ticket from last Friday.
- The email gateway is bouncing messages from that one domain again.
- Honestly that's the cleanest fix we've had all sprint.
- The support team needs read access to that log bucket.

Save the recording and send it back. No need to write anything else — just the audio file.
