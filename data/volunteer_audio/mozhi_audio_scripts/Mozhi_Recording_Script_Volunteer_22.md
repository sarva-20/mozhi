# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 22

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- The scheduled job overlapped with the backup window and locked the table.
- Someone accidentally disabled two-factor for the whole finance team.
- The config drifted between environments again after the last change.
- The API keeps timing out whenever the payload gets too large.
- The monitoring dashboard hasn't updated in almost twenty minutes.
- The encryption keys need to be rotated before the audit.
- Someone needs to update the runbook, half the steps are outdated.
- The IDE crashed twice while I was debugging that function.
- The nightly sync job failed silently for almost a week before anyone noticed.
- Can you restart the Docker container and see if that clears it up?
- The client's proxy is stripping headers our service depends on.
- We think the phishing email got past the filter because of a typo domain.
