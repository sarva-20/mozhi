# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 30

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- Someone needs to check why the antivirus keeps quarantining that installer.
- We should probably rotate the service account credentials this month.
- We think the phishing email got past the filter because of a typo domain.
- We think there was a credential stuffing attempt overnight.
- That's odd, the same request worked fine yesterday.
- The nightly sync job failed silently for almost a week before anyone noticed.
- We lost quorum on the cluster after that node went down.
- The token expired mid-session, so the upload silently failed.
- The contractor's laptop still hasn't been enrolled in device management.
- The client's proxy is stripping headers our service depends on.
- The ticket says the printer driver isn't installing on the new laptops.
- Someone should check if that outage affected billing as well.

## Part B — Say each of these acronyms out loud, the way you'd naturally say it

(One at a time is fine — you don't need to build a sentence around these.)

- JSON
- SNMP
- SDK
- CDN
- SSO
- POP3
- VLAN

## Submitting

Save the recording and send it back. No need to write anything else — just the audio file.
