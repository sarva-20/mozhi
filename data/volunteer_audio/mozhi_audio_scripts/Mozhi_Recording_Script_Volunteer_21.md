# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 21

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- The storage array is nearly full, we should plan an upgrade soon.
- We need a rollback plan in case the deployment goes sideways.
- We're not seeing the new build reflected on the staging server yet.
- Support said they'd follow up on the SLA breach by end of day.
- The dashboard filters reset every time you refresh the page.
- The access request is stuck in approval because the manager's out.
- We lost quorum on the cluster after that node went down.
- We should probably rotate the service account credentials this month.
- The WAN link to the branch office keeps dropping every afternoon.
- The support script doesn't handle accounts with special characters well.
- That's a fair point, we should raise it in tomorrow's standup.
- The shared calendar invite didn't sync to half the attendees.
