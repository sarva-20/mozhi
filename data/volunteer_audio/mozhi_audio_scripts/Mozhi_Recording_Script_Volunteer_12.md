# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 12

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- The client's proxy is stripping headers our service depends on.
- Can you confirm the new hire's badge access was set up correctly?
- The new hire still doesn't have access to the shared drive.
- I think we're hitting a rate limit on the third-party service.
- That fix works locally but breaks in the containerized environment.
- The onboarding checklist still references a tool we retired last year.
- I restarted the service but the queue is still backed up.
- The read replica fell behind after the schema change last night.
- The client's network team blocked our outbound requests by mistake.
- I think the certificate expired last night, that's why login is failing.
- The firewall rule change didn't get pushed to the branch office router.
- Someone needs to double check the retention policy before we delete anything.

Save the recording and send it back. No need to write anything else — just the audio file.
