# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 02

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- The scheduled job overlapped with the backup window and locked the table.
- We should stagger the update rollout instead of pushing it everywhere at once.
- The support script doesn't handle accounts with special characters well.
- The encryption keys need to be rotated before the audit.
- Can you check whether the load test numbers actually match production traffic?
- The onboarding script fails silently if the username has a space in it.
- That fix works locally but breaks in the containerized environment.
- We should probably automate that manual restart step.
- We think the phishing email got past the filter because of a typo domain.
- I restarted the service but the queue is still backed up.
- The test environment doesn't have the same data volume as production.
- The shared inbox rules got overwritten during the mailbox migration.
