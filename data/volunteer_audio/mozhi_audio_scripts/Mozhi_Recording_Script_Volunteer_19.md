# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 19

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- I think the outage was caused by a misconfigured load balancer rule.
- We should probably archive those old logs before the disk fills up.
- The contractor's laptop still hasn't been enrolled in device management.
- Someone should check if that outage affected billing as well.
- We need to update the DHCP settings on the new router.
- Someone should verify the disaster recovery site actually failed over correctly.
- That's a good catch, I hadn't thought about the edge case at all.
- That's honestly a cleaner way to structure the config files.
- That laptop keeps blue-screening every time it wakes from sleep.
- The new firewall policy is blocking traffic from our own office.
- That workaround is fine for now, but we should fix the root cause eventually.
- The onboarding script fails silently if the username has a space in it.
