# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 27

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- I think the outage was caused by a misconfigured load balancer rule.
- The ticket got auto-closed before anyone actually resolved it.
- The help desk is backed up because of the outage earlier.
- I restarted the service but the queue is still backed up.
- The client keeps getting logged out every fifteen minutes for no reason.
- Can we get someone from networking on this call, it's their subnet.
- The backup restored fine, but the indexes need to be rebuilt.
- We rolled back the release because the checkout flow was throwing errors.
- The report generator times out whenever the date range is too wide.
- We should stagger the update rollout instead of pushing it everywhere at once.
- Someone needs to double check the retention policy before we delete anything.
- The security team flagged a possible SQL injection attempt.
