# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 16

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- We think the outage was regional, not just our data center.
- We noticed the checksum didn't match after the file transfer.
- We're still waiting on the vendor to confirm the maintenance window.
- That's odd, the same request worked fine yesterday.
- The endpoint returns a 500 error whenever the request body is empty.
- The alert fired three times before anyone actually looked at it.
- The webhook stopped firing after we rotated the signing secret.
- The password reset emails are landing in everyone's spam folder.
- That's actually a really good idea, let's go with it.
- The dashboard widget keeps throwing an error on refresh.
- Can you check if the backup job actually completed last night?
- Someone needs to review the access logs from that suspicious login.

Save the recording and send it back. No need to write anything else — just the audio file.
