# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 11

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- The token expired mid-session, so the upload silently failed.
- The scanner keeps flagging that same false positive every week.
- The new laptop image still has the old antivirus client bundled in.
- We need to whitelist that IP before the integration test will pass.
- That's actually a really good idea, let's go with it.
- The support queue jumped overnight, must be tied to that outage.
- The build broke because a dependency wasn't resolved correctly.
- The support rep escalated it before even trying to reproduce the issue.
- The proxy is caching stale responses even after we cleared it.
- I think the outage was caused by a misconfigured load balancer rule.
- The ticket says the printer driver isn't installing on the new laptops.
- The report generator times out whenever the date range is too wide.

Save the recording and send it back. No need to write anything else — just the audio file.
