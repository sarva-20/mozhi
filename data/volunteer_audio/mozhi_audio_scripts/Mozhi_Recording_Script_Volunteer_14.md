# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 14

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- The email gateway is bouncing messages from that one domain again.
- That's actually the third time this exact ticket has been reopened.
- The security team flagged a possible SQL injection attempt.
- The build broke because a dependency wasn't resolved correctly.
- The mobile app crashes on launch for anyone on the older OS version.
- Someone left the debug logging turned on in prod, that's filling up the disk.
- The support rep escalated it before even trying to reproduce the issue.
- The new switch hasn't shown up in the network inventory yet.
- Someone needs to check why the antivirus keeps quarantining that installer.
- We think there was a credential stuffing attempt overnight.
- The support queue jumped overnight, must be tied to that outage.
- The escalation should have gone to tier two, not the front desk.
