# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 01

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- The vendor's status page still says everything is operational.
- The firewall rule change didn't get pushed to the branch office router.
- The client's firewall is dropping our health check pings intermittently.
- The new employee still can't print from the third floor.
- Support said they'd follow up on the SLA breach by end of day.
- Someone needs to patch that firmware before the weekend rollout.
- The security team flagged a possible SQL injection attempt.
- The deployment pipeline is stuck waiting on a manual approval step.
- Our uptime dropped below the promised threshold this quarter.
- That patch fixed the crash but introduced a weird rendering bug.
- That's odd, the same request worked fine yesterday.
- The nightly sync job failed silently for almost a week before anyone noticed.

Save the recording and send it back. No need to write anything else — just the audio file.
