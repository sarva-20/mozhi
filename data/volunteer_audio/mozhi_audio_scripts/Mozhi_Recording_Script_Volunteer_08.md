# Mozhi Recording Script — Volunteer 08

## How to record

- **Device:** Any phone voice-memo app works fine.
- **Format:** Save as `.m4a`, `.wav`, or `.mp3`.
- **Environment:** A normal room is fine — doesn't need to be silent.
- **Length:** One continuous recording, 2–3 minutes.
- **Pace:** Speak at your normal, everyday pace — don't slow down or over-pronounce. Say it like you're casually mentioning it to a coworker.
- **Mistakes:** If you stumble, just keep going or restart that one line — don't stop the recording.

## Part A — Read these sentences naturally, in order

- That's a good catch, I hadn't thought about the edge case at all.
- The VPN client won't connect from home for half the team today.
- We lost quorum on the cluster after that node went down.
- The endpoint returns a 500 error whenever the request body is empty.
- The alert fired three times before anyone actually looked at it.
- The dashboard filters reset every time you refresh the page.
- We should probably rotate the service account credentials this month.
- The client keeps getting logged out every fifteen minutes for no reason.
- The client's network team blocked our outbound requests by mistake.
- Someone should verify the disaster recovery site actually failed over correctly.
- We're not sure if the outage was on our end or the provider's.
- The IDE crashed twice while I was debugging that function.

## Part B — Say each of these acronyms out loud, the way you'd naturally say it

Save the recording and send it back. No need to write anything else — just the audio file.
